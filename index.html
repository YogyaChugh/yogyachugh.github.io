Episode 9AnyStudio

Building it now

Most no-code apps are just a website wrapped in an app.

So I'm building one that makes real native apps.

Meet AnyStudio.

Free to
start

ResultDescribe your app in plain words or drag it together, and AnyStudio turns it into a real native Android and iPhone app.

An AI helper builds screens with you, and you approve every change before it applies. Preview it live on your own phone, add sign-in, databases and payments that come built in, then publish under your own Google Play and App Store accounts. You own everything and can export the full source code at any time. Free to start; you upgrade when you're ready to publish.

Built with: Rust
